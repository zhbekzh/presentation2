Pre-understandings, put at risk

Theory and expectations

Expectations are pre-understandings I put at risk (Gadamer), not hypotheses to prove.

Politics of belonging

Yuval-Davis, 2006

“Russian” may appear as a label after losses or controversy; effort and gratitude may be named as conditions

Banal nationalism

Billig, 1995

Many comments may say “our” casually, with no conditions: a negative case to look for

Kazakhness and Kazakhstanness

Laruelle, 2014

Born-here athletes (Golovkin) may draw ethnic talk; brought-here athletes (Rybakina, Bublik) may draw passport and loyalty talk

Instrumental citizenship

Jansen et al., 2018; Han, Tang and Bairner, 2024

Support may rise and fall with results and contribution to the team

Gadamer (1960/2004), Truth and Method · Full references: A1

Working rung

“Ours” as fans use it

Any comment that claims, denies or conditions “ours” (наш / біздікі) for a named athlete

“Russian” gets its own code

the state

an ethnic group

language and culture

the athlete personally

Sartori’s ladder: a low rung keeps the concept from stretching. Feasible at a few hundred comments per event, coded by hand.

Risks and safeguards

Public comments are still people

No usernames or screenshots; quotes paraphrased or lightly altered

Hateful content

Report patterns; do not amplify slurs

Interviewees

Informed consent, voluntary, anonymous, adults only
